August 3rd

The day it began. I didn't know it yet, but my life quietly split into before you and after you. I remember how easy it felt, like I'd known you much longer than I had. Something in me just said: this one matters.

The evening at the port

I was nervous and trying very hard to look calm. I was so scared that you were going to kiss me that I kept looking away, at the water, at the boats, at anything but you, because my heart was beating so loud I was sure you could hear it. You probably noticed, and I think you liked it a little. Every time I glanced back, you were smiling, and every worry I had got a bit quieter. Then we went to your room and watched The Martian. Honestly, I barely remember the movie. I remember sitting next to you, how warm and comfortable it felt, and how I kept thinking: I don't want this evening to end. Somewhere between the port and that movie, I knew I wanted a hundred more nights exactly like this one, as long as they were with you.

The little everyday moments

It's not only the big days I keep going back to. It's the small ones. Laughing at something silly until we can't stop, talking about nothing for hours, the quiet when we're just together and it feels like enough. You turn the most ordinary moments into my favorite ones. And if today feels heavy, I want you to know that the sadness doesn't change a single thing about how wonderful you are. I'm still here, I'm still yours, and I'd happily sit beside you through every cloudy day until the sun comes back. It always does, and I'll be right there when it does.

Today

You're the first person I think of when I wake up and the last one before I fall asleep. You've become my favorite part of every day, and I'm so grateful it's you.
